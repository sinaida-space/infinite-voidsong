Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-19

Infinite Voidsong is built to need as little of your data as possible. There is no analytics, no cookies, and no request ever leaves your browser except to load the page itself.

Who runs this

The data controller is Sinaida Krivchenko, based in Prague, Czech Republic. For any privacy question, reach out through sinaida.eu.

What is stored, and where

Infinite Voidsong saves your mixer settings and preferences in your browser's own storage, under a single key:

  • voidsong:v1, holding layer volumes, the active preset, timer choice, and whether you have dismissed this notice.

This stays on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, never read by anyone else, and clearing your browser storage removes it completely.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies.
  • No third-party requests: fonts, icons, and every asset are self-hosted.
  • No accounts, no forms, no advertising.
  • No automated decision-making or profiling.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel Inc. processes standard connection logs (such as IP address and request timestamp) as a processor, to serve the site and keep it secure. This may involve processing outside the European Union. Their practices are described in the Vercel privacy policy.

Your rights

Since nothing is stored outside your own browser, there is no remote record to request, correct, or delete: clearing your browser storage does that instantly. For connection logs processed by Vercel through hosting, you may still exercise your rights under GDPR (access, correction, deletion, objection) by contacting sinaida.eu, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
