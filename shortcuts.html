Keyboard shortcuts

Every shortcut works while the mixer has focus.

  • SpacePlay / pause
  • ↑ / ↓Master volume up / down
  • ← / →Move between layers
  • 1Preset: Deep Focus
  • 2Preset: Reading & Writing
  • 3Preset: Creative Flow
  • 4Preset: Routine
  • 5Preset: Break & Restore
  • 6Preset: Sleep
  • MMute the selected layer
  • EscClose the open panel
